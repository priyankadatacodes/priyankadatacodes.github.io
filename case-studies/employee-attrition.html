Employee Attrition Analysis & Prediction

End-to-end HR analytics project using Python, SQL, Machine Learning, and Power BI

← Back to People Analytics

Executive Summary

Employee attrition directly impacts organizational cost, productivity, and workforce stability. This project delivers an end-to-end employee attrition analysis to help HR and leadership teams identify high-risk employee segments and take proactive retention actions.

Why I Built This Project

Even small attrition rates can result in significant hiring and training costs. I built this project to understand why employees leave, identify attrition patterns, and practice analyzing HR data from a business decision-making perspective.

Business Context

  • High attrition increases recruitment and onboarding costs
  • Knowledge loss impacts productivity and team continuity
  • HR teams need clarity on high-risk employee segments
  • Retention efforts must be focused, not generic

Problem Statement

Analyze employee-level HR data to measure attrition rate, identify key drivers of employee turnover, and highlight high-risk employee segments to support data-driven retention strategies.

Dataset Overview

  • Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset
  • Granularity: Individual employee-level records
  • Target variable: Attrition (Yes / No)

Tools Used

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Scikit-learn · MySQL · SQL · Power BI

Analysis Approach

  • Data cleaning and validation using Python
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  • Logistic Regression modeling for attrition drivers
  • SQL-based KPI validation
  • Power BI dashboard creation for leadership reporting

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Employees
  • Attrited Employees
  • Attrition Rate (%)
  • Average Monthly Income
  • Average Employee Tenure

Key Insights

  • Overall attrition rate is ~16%
  • Higher attrition among early-career employees
  • Lower income bands show higher turnover risk
  • Sales and R&D contribute the most attrition
  • Overtime and frequent travel increase attrition likelihood

Business Impact

  • Enables early identification of high-risk employees
  • Supports focused and cost-effective retention planning
  • Helps reduce hiring and training costs
  • Improves workforce stability
