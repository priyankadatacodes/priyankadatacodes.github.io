Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

End-to-end churn analysis and prediction using SQL, Python, and Power BI

← Back to Customer Analytics

Executive Summary

This project analyzes customer churn in the telecommunications industry to identify high-risk customers, understand key churn drivers, and support data-driven retention strategies. The company is experiencing a churn rate of ~26.6%, which poses a significant risk to long-term revenue. Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, the analysis highlights churn patterns and customers most likely to leave.

Why I Built This Project

Customer churn is one of the most expensive problems in subscription-based businesses. Acquiring new customers costs significantly more than retaining existing ones. This project mirrors a real-world scenario where a data analyst supports revenue and retention decisions using both descriptive and predictive analytics.

Business Context

  • Telecom companies rely on recurring monthly revenue.
  • High competition and low switching costs increase churn risk.
  • High churn leads to revenue loss and increased acquisition costs.
  • Early identification of churn risk enables proactive retention.

Problem Statement

Analyze telecom customer data to measure churn, identify key churn drivers, predict high-risk customers, and support targeted retention strategies with maximum business impact.

Dataset Overview

Telecom customer dataset containing 7,043 records with customer demographics, service usage, contract details, tenure, billing information, and churn status.

Tools Used

SQL (MySQL) · Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn) · Power BI · Jupyter Notebook

Analysis Approach

  • Imported and stored raw data in MySQL
  • Performed data cleaning and EDA using Python
  • Identified churn drivers through statistical analysis
  • Built Logistic Regression and Random Forest models
  • Created Power BI dashboards for business reporting

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Customers
  • Churn Rate (%)
  • Retained vs Churned Customers
  • Average Tenure (Months)
  • Customer Lifetime Value Indicators

Key Insights

  • Overall churn rate is ~26.6%.
  • Customers with low tenure are significantly more likely to churn.
  • Higher monthly charges increase churn probability.
  • Month-to-month contracts show higher churn than long-term contracts.
  • ~6% of customers are identified as high-risk and should be prioritized.

Business Impact

  • Enables proactive churn prevention.
  • Supports targeted retention instead of mass campaigns.
  • Helps protect customer lifetime value.
  • Reduces revenue leakage from early churn.
