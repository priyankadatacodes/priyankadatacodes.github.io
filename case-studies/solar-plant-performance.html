Solar Plant Performance & Efficiency Analysis

Tools: Python, SQL (MySQL), Power BI

End-to-end analysis of solar power plant operational data to understand energy generation, efficiency, system-level loss, and inverter performance. This project focuses on turning raw sensor data into clear KPIs and insights that support operational and management decisions.

Why This Project

Solar plants generate large volumes of sensor data every day, but raw data alone is difficult to interpret and not useful for quick decision-making. Operations teams need simple metrics and trends to understand plant health, efficiency, and performance issues.

Business Problem

  • How much energy is the plant generating?
  • How efficient is the system overall?
  • Where is energy being lost?
  • Is plant performance degrading over time?
  • Which inverters need attention?

Dataset Overview

  • Time-series solar generation and weather data
  • Metrics: DC power, AC power, daily energy yield
  • Weather data: irradiation, ambient temperature, module temperature
  • Inverter-level operational records

Data Preparation

  • Loaded generation and weather datasets using Python
  • Converted timestamps into datetime format
  • Removed duplicate and invalid records
  • Filtered non-operational data (DC power > 0 and AC power > 0)
  • Created derived columns required for analysis

Analysis Approach

  • Cleaned and prepared data using Pandas and NumPy
  • Stored cleaned data in MySQL for structured querying
  • Connected Power BI directly to the database
  • Created DAX measures for KPIs and trends
  • Built a single-page executive dashboard

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Energy Generated (kWh)
  • Average Panel Efficiency (%)
  • System Energy Utilization Loss (%)
  • Peak Daily Energy Output (kWh)
  • Average Daily Energy Yield

Note: System Energy Utilization Loss represents downtime, curtailment, and non-generation periods, not inverter conversion loss.

Key Insights

  • A large portion of energy loss is driven by non-generating periods and downtime
  • Overall system efficiency remains relatively stable across time
  • Monthly AC power trends help identify early degradation patterns
  • Some inverters consistently perform below the plant average

Impact

  • Converted complex solar sensor data into clear KPIs that are easy to understand
  • Helped teams quickly see where and why energy loss was happening
  • Made it easier to identify underperforming inverters for timely maintenance
  • Enabled tracking of monthly performance trends to catch degradation early
  • Delivered a single-page Power BI dashboard for daily monitoring and reporting

Final Takeaway

This project shows how solar plant operational data can be transformed into simple, actionable insights. Instead of working with raw data, teams can use clear KPIs and dashboards to monitor performance and support better decisions.
