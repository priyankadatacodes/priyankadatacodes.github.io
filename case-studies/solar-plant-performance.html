Solar Plant Performance & Efficiency Analysis

End-to-end analysis of solar plant generation, efficiency, and energy loss using Python, SQL, and Power BI

← Back to Energy Projects

Executive Summary

This project analyzes solar power plant generation data to understand energy production, system efficiency, and operational energy loss. Python was used for data cleaning, MySQL for data storage, and Power BI for building an executive-level dashboard. The dashboard provides a clear view of plant performance, trends, and underperforming inverters.

Why I Built This Project

Solar plants generate large volumes of operational data, but raw data alone is not useful for decision-making. I built this project to practice real-world energy analytics and demonstrate how raw sensor data can be converted into meaningful KPIs and insights for business and operations teams.

Business Context

  • How much energy is the plant generating?
  • How efficient is the solar plant overall?
  • How much energy is being lost at the system level?
  • Is plant performance degrading over time?
  • Which inverters require operational attention?

Dataset Overview

Time-series solar plant generation and weather data including DC power, AC power, daily energy yield, irradiation, ambient temperature, module temperature, and inverter-level identifiers.

Tools Used

Python · Pandas · NumPy · MySQL · SQLAlchemy · Power BI

Data Preparation

  • Loaded generation and weather datasets
  • Converted timestamps to datetime format
  • Removed duplicates and invalid values
  • Filtered non-operational records (DC > 0 and AC > 0)
  • Stored cleaned data in MySQL for analysis and reporting

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Energy Generated (kWh)
  • Average Panel Efficiency (%)
  • System Energy Utilization Loss (%)
  • Peak Daily Energy Output
  • Average Daily Specific Yield

Key Insights

  • A large portion of energy loss comes from non-generating periods and downtime.
  • System-level efficiency remains relatively stable.
  • Monthly AC power trends help identify performance degradation.
  • Some inverters consistently perform below plant average.

Business Impact

  • Helps operators quickly understand plant performance.
  • Supports early identification of underperforming inverters.
  • Improves maintenance planning and operational decision-making.
