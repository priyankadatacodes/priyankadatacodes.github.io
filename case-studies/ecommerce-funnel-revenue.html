E-Commerce Funnel & Revenue Leakage Analysis

Order-level funnel performance, revenue realization, and delivery analytics using Python, SQL, and Tableau

← Back to Portfolio

Executive Summary

This project analyzes the end-to-end e-commerce order funnel using the Brazilian OLIST dataset, focusing on order completion, revenue leakage, delivery timelines, and seller performance. The analysis shows that the overall funnel is strong with minimal revenue loss due to cancellations, while delivery delays vary across sellers and impact customer experience at scale.

Why I Built This Project

Large e-commerce platforms handle thousands of orders daily across multiple sellers. Even when conversion looks healthy, post-purchase issues such as delivery delays or cancellations can affect customer trust and long-term business value. This project mirrors a real-world operations analytics scenario.

Business Context

  • Marketplace platforms depend on multiple sellers.
  • Delivery timelines directly affect customer experience.
  • Small inefficiencies can scale into large operational issues.
  • Business teams need visibility into post-purchase funnel health.

Problem Statement

Analyze order-level e-commerce data to evaluate funnel performance, quantify revenue loss due to cancellations, assess delivery timelines, and identify seller-level operational issues impacting customer experience and efficiency.

Dataset Overview

Brazilian OLIST E-commerce Dataset (Order-level data including orders, order items, products, and delivery timestamps). Only relevant tables were used to keep analysis focused on business outcomes.

Tools Used

Python · MySQL · SQL · Tableau

Analysis Approach

  • Cleaned and prepared raw CSV data using Python
  • Loaded cleaned data into MySQL
  • Analyzed funnel performance and cancellations using SQL
  • Evaluated delivery timelines and seller-level performance
  • Built a single-page Tableau dashboard for business reporting

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Orders
  • Funnel Conversion Rate
  • Cancellation Rate
  • Total Revenue
  • Revenue Loss Percentage
  • Average Delivery Time (Days)

Key Insights

  • Most orders are delivered successfully, indicating a healthy funnel.
  • Revenue loss due to cancellations is minimal.
  • Delivery delays are not strongly linked to cancellations.
  • A small group of sellers consistently delivers slower than average.
  • Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of product categories.

Business Impact

  • Confirms that revenue leakage is not a major business risk.
  • Highlights delivery performance as the key improvement area.
  • Supports seller-level monitoring to improve customer experience.
