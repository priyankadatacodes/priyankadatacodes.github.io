AI Search & Zero-Click Impact on E-commerce

Funnel, conversion, and revenue impact analysis of AI-powered search and zero-click behavior

← Back to Portfolio

Executive Summary

This project analyzes how AI-powered search and zero-click experiences impact conversion rate, revenue, and funnel performance in an e-commerce business. While AI Search drives higher and more consistent revenue, a noticeable portion of users exit through zero-click behavior, never entering the purchase funnel. The largest revenue leakage occurs between Click → Add-to-Cart, highlighting a key optimization opportunity.

Why I Built This Project

AI Search and zero-click experiences are now common across modern e-commerce platforms. However, many teams struggle to quantify whether these features improve revenue or silently reduce product discovery. This project was built to measure the real business impact using data instead of assumptions.

Business Context

  • AI-powered search results and generated answers are becoming standard.
  • Zero-click experiences can shorten the shopping journey.
  • Reduced exploration can lead to silent revenue leakage.
  • The key question is whether AI Search is a growth lever or a monetization risk.

Problem Statement

Analyze session-level e-commerce data to measure the impact of AI Search and zero-click behavior on conversion rate, revenue, and funnel performance, and identify opportunities to improve revenue without increasing traffic acquisition costs.

Tools Used

SQL · Python (Pandas) · Tableau

Analysis Approach

  • Data cleaning and validation using Python
  • Funnel, conversion, and revenue analysis using SQL
  • Validation of KPIs using Python
  • Dashboard creation in Tableau for stakeholders

Key KPIs Tracked

  • Total Sessions
  • Orders
  • Conversion Rate
  • Total Revenue
  • Add-to-Cart Rate
  • Zero-Click Percentage

Key Insights

  • Overall conversion rate is ~10%, indicating strong user intent.
  • ~18% of sessions end as zero-click, never entering the funnel.
  • The biggest revenue drop occurs before Add-to-Cart.
  • AI Search generates higher and more consistent revenue than classic search.
  • Zero-click behavior limits product discovery and monetization.

Business Impact

  • Improving Click → Add-to-Cart can unlock significant revenue.
  • Reducing zero-click dead ends increases funnel depth.
  • Revenue growth is possible without increasing marketing spend.
